Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

1. Who we are

Éclat Narrative is a branding and social media agency based at Shalimar Oel, New Hyderabad, Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh 226006, India. This policy explains what personal information we collect through eclatnarrative.com and how we use it.

2. What we collect

We only collect information you choose to give us:

  • Contact form: your name, email address, phone number and message.
  • Email and WhatsApp: whatever you include when you write to us directly.

We do not use analytics, advertising trackers or cookies on this website.

3. How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and discuss your project.
  • To prepare proposals and, if you become a client, to deliver our services.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information.

4. Services we rely on

A few third-party services handle data when you use this website. Each has its own privacy policy:

  • FormSubmit delivers contact form messages to our inbox.
  • Google hosts our email (Gmail) and serves the fonts on this website, which means your browser sends your IP address to Google when a page loads.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) handles messages you send us on WhatsApp.
  • Instagram and LinkedIn links take you to those platforms, where their own policies apply.

5. Work shown on this website

Our portfolio shows work we created for our clients. If you appear in any of it and would like it removed, email us and we will take it down.

6. How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for as long as we need them to respond and follow up, and then delete them. Client records are kept for the length of our engagement and for as long as the law requires afterwards.

7. Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you;
  • correct or update it;
  • delete it; or
  • stop using it, where you gave us consent.

To make a request, email eclatnarrative@gmail.com. We will respond as soon as we reasonably can.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you send us. No method of sending or storing data over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children

This website is intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it last changed.
